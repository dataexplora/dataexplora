- 🎼 Trained musician: Integrated Master in Music Studies (AUTh), thesis on AI melody harmonization
- 📞 These days I'm deep in **voice AI**: agents that pick up real business phones in Greek
- 🤖 I run my own company with a team of AI agents and a memory system I built for them
- 🇬🇷 Building from Athens, for Greek businesses first

### 🏆 Things I've shipped

- 📞 **[LEXIS AI](https://lexisai.gr)** · AI phone agents live on real Greek business lines. I built the whole stack: our own cloud PBX, the voice agents, an outbound dialer, self-serve onboarding, billing and invoicing
- 🧾 **[Λογιστής AI](https://logistis-ai.gr)** · Greek tax answers in seconds, each cited to the actual law. Paying customers
- 📣 **[Brandefer](https://brandefer.com)** · Social media management with an MCP layer, so AI agents can publish and reply across networks
- ⛽ **On-premise AI for a fuel distributor** · Orders arrive by email, AI reads and translates them, trucks get routed. Built solo
- 🏢 **[AIGENTA](https://aigenta.gr)** · The company behind all of it, now incorporated as a US LLC
