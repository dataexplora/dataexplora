### Who I am

I trained as a musician. I spent years learning to hear timing, tone and the space between notes. Then I started building machines that listen, and I never stopped.

Today I start companies. I take an idea from a blank page to something real businesses pay for, and I stay close enough to pick up the phone when a customer calls.

### Where I'm strong

🎯 **Zero to production.** I carry an idea all the way: the product, the infrastructure, the billing, the support. No handoffs, no waiting for a team to exist.

🎧 **Conversation.** My ear came from music. I hear when a voice agent sounds wrong before any metric does, and I design for how people actually talk.

🤖 **Agents that do real work.** Not demos. Agents on real phone lines and real inboxes, with real customers on the other end.

📣 **Distribution.** 200M views on AI content and #1 globally on TikTok. I know how to get attention and turn it into conversations.

🤝 **Selling.** I talk to customers myself. What gets built next comes from those calls, not from a roadmap.

⚙️ **Running a company with AI.** Most of my day-to-day operations run on agents and memory systems I built for myself.

### How I work

- Ship to a real customer first, polish second
- Listen to the customer's words, not my assumptions
- The simple solution, unless the hard one earns it
- Own the outcome end to end

### Talk to me about

Voice AI · agentic systems · building in Greece for the world · going from zero to one
